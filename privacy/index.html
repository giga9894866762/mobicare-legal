Privacy Policy

Mobicare, Mobicare Vendor and Mobicare Admin
Last updated: 27 September 2026

Mobicare is a marketplace for mobile phone accessories, operated by Graphin. This policy explains what we collect in our three apps, why, and what you can do about it. It covers:

  • Mobicare (com.mobicares) — the shopping app for customers
  • Mobicare Vendor (com.mobivendor) — for approved businesses selling on Mobicare
  • Mobicare Admin (com.reginalmoatmin) — an internal app for Graphin staff

The short version. We collect what we need to take your order and deliver it. We do not sell your information, we do not share it with advertisers, and there are no adverts or tracking services in any of our apps. We do not take card or bank details, because orders are paid in cash on delivery.

What we collect

Mobicare (customers)

InformationWhy we need it
Name and email addressTo identify your account. These come from your Graphin sign-in
Phone number and delivery addressTo deliver your order and let the courier reach you
Orders, cart and wishlistTo process orders and show you your own history
Photos you choose to uploadOnly if you attach one, for example to a return request. The app asks for camera access at that moment and you can refuse
A notification identifier for your deviceTo tell you when your order status changes. You can turn notifications off

Mobicare Vendor (sellers)

The same account details as above, plus your business name and store details, the products you list, the photos you upload of them, and the orders placed with your store. Camera access is used for photographing products.

Mobicare Admin (Graphin staff)

Name and email address only, to confirm you are a member of staff and which region you are responsible for. Staff using this app see customer and vendor records belonging to their own region in order to do their job.

What we do not collect

  • No card, bank or payment details. Orders are paid in cash on delivery, so there is nothing to take
  • No location tracking. We use the delivery address you type, not your device's position
  • No advertising identifier, and no advertising or analytics services of any kind
  • No contacts, no call or message history, no health data
  • No microphone or audio recording

How we use it

Only to run the service: to sign you in, show you the catalogue, take and deliver your order, let you cancel or return something, answer you if you contact us, notify you about your own orders, and keep the service secure and working. We do not use your information to build advertising profiles, and we do not make automated decisions about you.

Who else sees it

We do not sell your information and we do not share it for advertising. It is seen only by:

  • The seller of what you ordered, and only what is needed to fulfil it — your name, delivery address and phone number, and the items. They do not see your other orders
  • The courier delivering it, for the same reason
  • Graphin staff, where their job requires it, limited to their own region
  • Our service providers, listed below, who process data on our instructions and for no purpose of their own
  • The authorities, if the law requires us to hand something over

How it is protected

Everything sent between the app and our servers travels over an encrypted connection. Sign-in is handled by a dedicated identity service rather than by the app holding your password. Access by staff is limited by role and by region. No system is perfectly secure, but if a breach affects you we will tell you and the relevant authority as the law requires.

How long we keep it

Your account details are kept while your account is open. Order records are kept for as long as we are required to keep business and tax records, because an order is also a receipt. When we no longer need something, we delete it or strip it of anything that identifies you.

Your choices and rights

  • See or correct your details — from your profile in the app, or ask us
  • Get a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Delete your account and the personal information in it — ask us and we will do it, keeping only what the law requires us to retain for completed orders
  • Turn off notifications — in the app, or in your phone's settings
  • Refuse camera access — the rest of the app works without it
  • Complain to your local data protection authority if you are unhappy with how we responded

To do any of these, write to us using the address below. We reply within 30 days.

Children

These apps are for adults, and none of them is aimed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has given us their details, write to us and we will remove them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and for anything significant we will tell you in the app before it takes effect.
