The short version. Email us from the address on your account and ask us to delete it. We confirm your identity, delete what we can straight away, and tell you what — if anything — we have to keep and for how long.

How to request deletion

  1. Email giga9894866762@gmail.com from the same email address you sign in with, with the subject "Delete my account"
  2. Tell us which app your account is on — Mobicare, Mobicare Vendor, or Mobicare Admin
  3. We confirm it's really you, then delete your account and personal data
  4. We reply once it's done — usually within a few days, and always within 30 days

There is no in-app delete-account button yet. Email is the only way to request it, for every app.

What gets deleted

AppDeleted
Mobicare (customers)Your name, delivery addresses, saved photos, wishlist, cart, and your sign-in link to the account. Your profile no longer appears anywhere in the app
Mobicare VendorYour business profile, store details and product photos. Product listings are taken off the marketplace
Mobicare AdminYour staff profile and access is revoked immediately

What we keep, and why

Completed orders are also financial records, so we keep the order itself — items, amount, and date — for as long as tax and business-record law requires us to, the same way a paper receipt would be kept. We remove your name, address and contact details from those records where we can, so the record no longer identifies you personally.

If you have an open or in-progress order at the time you ask, we finish handling that order (delivery, return, or refund) before deleting the rest of your account.

Timing

Your profile, addresses, photos and sign-in access are removed as soon as we confirm your request — normally within a few days. Anything we're required to keep for business or tax records follows the retention period above rather than being deleted immediately.
